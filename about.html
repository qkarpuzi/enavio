About

What Enavio AI actually is.

Enavio AI designs document intelligence and AI assistant systems — mostly OCR/classification and retrieval-augmented chat grounded in real organizational data. Currently building for construction, with more industries in scope as the work grows.

We don't build AI for the sake of AI.

Right now, that means turning documents nobody wants to read into answers people can actually use. Not a full-stack AI agency covering everything — a focused practice on reading documents and answering questions from real data.

OCR & Document Classification Retrieval-Augmented Generation Institutional AI Assistants Data Extraction & Structuring

How we build these systems.

Three specific things we hold ourselves to, not slogans.

01

Grounded, not guessed

An AI assistant is only useful if it doesn't make things up. Every system we build answers from your actual documents and records — not general internet knowledge.

02

Tested on messy input

A document classifier that only works on clean scans isn't a real product. We test against blurry photos, inconsistent formats, and the actual paperwork people deal with.

03

Built to fit in

These systems plug into how an organization already works — existing document stores, existing dashboards — instead of asking anyone to change tools.

Engineering philosophy.

A system starts as a problem worth solving, not a model looking for a use case.
