Services

What we build, and how.

Not a full-stack AI agency covering everything. Four related capabilities we go deep on — each engineered around an organization's real documents and data, not general AI guesswork.

01

Document Intelligence

OCR and automatic classification systems that read scanned documents — or even photos of them — extract the text, and sort them by type without manual filing.

  • Digitizing paper archives into searchable, structured records
  • Auto-sorting inbound documents (invoices, contracts, forms) by type
  • Extracting key fields from documents for downstream systems

02

RAG-Grounded AI Assistants

Chat assistants that answer only from your organization's actual documents and records — retrieved and cited at answer time, not recalled from general training data.

  • Student- or staff-facing assistants grounded in institutional policy
  • Internal knowledge assistants for procedures and documentation
  • Support assistants that cite the exact source document used

03

Operational Risk & Anomaly Detection

Systems that cross-reference operational records — like invoices against project data — to flag mismatches and at-risk items automatically, before someone finds them the hard way.

  • Cross-referencing invoices against project budgets or contracts
  • Flagging records that don't reconcile across two data sources
  • Surfacing at-risk items on a dashboard instead of a spreadsheet
